I · The Origin

In the beginning
there was
only mist.

Hydrogen. Oxygen. Two atoms bonded by heat and time — forming the molecule that would birth all life on Earth.

II

The deep
remembers.

3.8 billion years ago, water pooled in the dark. No light reached the ocean floor. Life stirred in silence — chemistry becoming biology, becoming memory.

97%
of Earth's water is ocean
III

Rivers run
like thought —
always downward.

117 major rivers carve their paths through stone. Not by force — by patience. A river does not fight the mountain; it finds every yielding crack, wearing the world away drop by drop.

0.0001%
of water is in rivers — yet it feeds billions
IV

Ice is water
holding still,
remembering cold.

When water freezes, it expands — unique among molecules. It cracks rock, carves valleys, and forms the great glaciers that have shaped every continent on Earth.

69%
of freshwater locked in glaciers
V

"The water you drink today was once a glacier. Was once a cloud. Was once inside a dinosaur."

The water cycle has no beginning and no end. Evaporation, condensation, precipitation — a loop running without pause for 4 billion years.

VI

We turned
water into
a question.

2.2 billion people lack access to safe water. We pollute, we dam, we overpump. The system that sustained life for eons bends under a single century of human pressure.

2030
projected year of global water deficit

VII

Water will always
find its way.
Will we?

In desalination, in atmospheric harvesting, in rewilded watersheds — the answers exist. Water teaches us: move gently, persist quietly, and you will carve stone.
