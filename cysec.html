🇹🇭 Thailand Survival Guide

🚫 Prohibitions & Conduct

  • No Alcohol/Drugs: Strict Drug Laws with severe penalties.
  • Curfew: In rooms by 10:30 PM. Random checks conducted.
  • No Smoking: Strictly not allowed on campus.
  • No Harassment: Zero-tolerance policy.
  • No Shouting: Prohibited in dorms and public areas.
  • No Stealing/Theft: Do not take unlocked cycles. CCTVs are active. Shoplifting has severe legal penalties.
  • Behavior: No humiliating, pranks, or bullying. No public intimacies.
  • Cleanliness: Use dustbins, do not litter.
  • Personal Issues: Do NOT involve volunteers in handling personal problems (e.g. couple fights).

👔 Dress & Attendance

  • Classes: Smart formal dress. Attendance is taken daily and sent to your university. Be on time and keep phones on silent.
  • Field Trips: Casual dress. Do NOT go by yourselves (stay in groups). Carry digital copies of passports, not originals.
  • Temples: Dress properly (no revealing clothes), take shoes off before entering. Ladies must NOT touch a Buddhist monk.

🏠 Accommodation Rules

  • Turn off AC & Lights before leaving. Damages borne by occupant.
  • No cooking (electric kettles & microwaves allowed).

🇹🇭 Thai Culture Tips

  • Use the title "Khun" before first names for adults.
  • The Royal Family is held in great reverence; do not speak disrespectfully of them.
  • Dining: Knives aren't necessary (food is pre-cut). Tipping 10-20 Baht is appreciated.

💰 Currency & Banking

  • Currency: Thai Baht (THB). ~30 THB = 1 USD. Max 50,000 THB can be taken out without authorization.
  • Banks: Kasikorn (K Bank) & Siam Commercial (SCB) near AIT.

🏥 Facilities & Telecom

  • Facilities: Medical Clinic, Swimming Pool, Tennis Court, Beauty/Massage Parlor, Prayer Rooms (near MEB).
  • Calling Home: 30% surcharge from hotel rooms! Buy SIM cards at AIT Bookstore.
  • Apps to Download: Grab, Bolt, ANYWHEEL (for e-bikes), Google Translate.

🔌 Utilities

  • Electricity: 220V 50Hz. Bring 2-pin adapters.
  • Water: Refilling stations cost 1 Baht/L. Drink bottled/boiled water.
  • Laundry: Vending machines in front of Dorm L, ST6, ST9, ST11.

🚌 Transport & Meals

  • Green e-bikes in AIT (Use ANYWHEEL app).
  • Bus 29 & 510, Cabs, or Bolt/Grab off-campus.
  • BTS (Sky Train) / MRT (Metro) available near Future Park.
  • Meals: Free basic meals (Breakfast, lunch, dinner) are provided for the entire program!

🛒 Nearby (AIT)

  • Food: AIT Cafe, 7-Eleven, Hom Krun, Tan90, UFM Bakery.
  • Future Park: 10km away. Take Bus 29, 39, 510 or Grab.
  • Thammasat Univ: TUH Plaza/Square. Night market on Mon/Thu.
  • Supermarkets: MAKRO, TESCO LOTUS, CARREFOUR, ZEER RANGSIT (IT).

🌆 Bangkok Malls (BTS access)

  • ICONSIAM: Luxury shopping on the river.
  • MBK Center: Knockoffs & authentic goods (National Stadium Station).
  • Siam Paragon: High-end fashion & Aquarium (Siam Station).
  • Platinum Fashion Mall: Wholesale clothing (Pratunam). Haggling encouraged!
  • Pantip Plaza: Famous electronics mall (Chidlom Station).
  • Central World / Siam Discovery: Upscale shopping.
  • One Bangkok: Thailand's largest integrated luxury district.
  • Central Chidlom & Gaysorn: High-end luxury designer goods.

💡 Shopping Tips

  • Bargaining: Practiced in markets (Platinum, MBK). Ask for discounts politely.
  • Receipts: Always get receipts, especially a certificate of guarantee for jewelry.
