Reflections

What's something new you've experienced lately? How did that feel?

Riding a zipline, in Madhuri Lake, was a fascinating experience, ravishing in its own way — the rush of the descent balanced by the vast, unspoiled beauty below. The lake itself, with its surreal stillness and high-altitude charm, adds a poetic contrast to the speed of the ride.

A recent challenge you faced? How are you working to overcome it?

Completing Signal and Systems. Breaking down problems into small, intuitive steps. Sometimes visualizing signals as waves or using practical analogies — like filtering sound in music production — can make abstract ideas more graspable.

What's something in your life that you're proud of right now?

Completing the Arunachal Pradesh Tour — an adventure carried through with heart and wonder.

How do you find inspiration when you're looking for the right aesthetic?

Whether I'm tweaking cinematic AI prompts or capturing the moody, misty landscapes of Arunachal, it’s all about chasing a specific vibe. Finding that perfect blend of light, shadow, and atmosphere feels like catching lightning in a bottle.

When did you recently feel a strong sense of community?

Organizing the Saraswati Puja this year for the local committee back in Khardah. Managing the itinerary, sorting the bills, and handling the promotional stuff was definitely hectic, but seeing everyone come together made the chaos completely worth it.

Where do you go to just unwind and disconnect?

Either grinding out tactical formations in FC Mobile, climbing ranks in Brawl Stars, or just appreciating the smooth performance of my new Ryzen 5 setup. It's my digital sanctuary after a long day of coding edge hardware and classes.

What is a feeling from your travels you want to hold onto?

That overwhelming sense of scale. Standing among the mountains and monasteries puts everything into perspective. It's a quiet reminder to keep things simple and move through life with a lighter heart.
