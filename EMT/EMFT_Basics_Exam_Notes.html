1. The Three Orthogonal Coordinate Systems

Cartesian Coordinates $(x, y, z)$

Ranges: $-\infty < x < \infty, \; -\infty < y < \infty, \; -\infty < z < \infty$

$$d\mathbf{l} = dx\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_x + dy\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_y + dz\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$ $$dv = dx\,dy\,dz$$ $$d\mathbf{S}_x = dy\,dz\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_x, \quad d\mathbf{S}_y = dx\,dz\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_y, \quad d\mathbf{S}_z = dx\,dy\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$

Cylindrical Coordinates $(\rho, \phi, z)$

Ranges: $0 \le \rho < \infty, \; 0 \le \phi < 2\pi, \; -\infty < z < \infty$

$$d\mathbf{l} = d\rho\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\rho + \rho\,d\phi\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi + dz\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$ $$dv = \rho\,d\rho\,d\phi\,dz$$ $$d\mathbf{S}_\rho = \rho\,d\phi\,dz\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\rho, \quad d\mathbf{S}_\phi = d\rho\,dz\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi, \quad d\mathbf{S}_z = \rho\,d\rho\,d\phi\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$

Spherical Coordinates $(r, \theta, \phi)$

Ranges: $0 \le r < \infty, \; 0 \le \theta \le \pi, \; 0 \le \phi < 2\pi$

$$d\mathbf{l} = dr\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_r + r\,d\theta\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\theta + r\sin\theta\,d\phi\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi$$ $$dv = r^2\sin\theta\,dr\,d\theta\,d\phi$$ $$d\mathbf{S}_r = r^2\sin\theta\,d\theta\,d\phi\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_r, \quad d\mathbf{S}_\theta = r\sin\theta\,dr\,d\phi\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\theta, \quad d\mathbf{S}_\phi = r\,dr\,d\theta\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi$$

3. Vector Line, Surface, and Volume Integrals

Line Integral

Circulation / Work done along path $L$:

$$\int_L \mathbf{A}\cdot d\mathbf{l}$$

Closed line circulation: $\oint_L \mathbf{A}\cdot d\mathbf{l}$

Surface Integral (Flux)

Total outward flux $\Psi$ through surface $S$:

$$\Psi = \int_S \mathbf{A}\cdot d\mathbf{S}$$

Net outward flux from closed surface: $\oint_S \mathbf{A}\cdot d\mathbf{S}$

Volume Integral

Total charge $Q$ enclosed within volume $v$:

$$Q = \int_v \rho_v \, dv$$

4. The Vector Differential DEL Operator ($\nabla$)

Cartesian

$$\nabla = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_x + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_y + \frac{\partial}{\partial z}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$

Cylindrical

$$\nabla = \hat{\mathbf{a}}_\rho \frac{\partial}{\partial \rho} + \hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi \frac{1}{\rho}\frac{\partial}{\partial \phi} + \hat{\mathbf{a}}_z \frac{\partial}{\partial z}$$

Spherical

$$\nabla = \hat{\mathbf{a}}_r \frac{\partial}{\partial r} + \hat{\mathbf{a}}_\theta \frac{1}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} + \hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi \frac{1}{r\sin\theta}\frac{\partial}{\partial \phi}$$

5. Gradient of a Scalar Field ($\nabla V$)

The gradient of a scalar field $V$ is a vector that points in the direction of the maximum rate of increase of $V$, with magnitude equal to that maximum rate.

Cartesian Coordinates

$$\nabla V = \frac{\partial V}{\partial x}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_x + \frac{\partial V}{\partial y}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_y + \frac{\partial V}{\partial z}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$

Cylindrical Coordinates

$$\nabla V = \frac{\partial V}{\partial \rho}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\rho + \frac{1}{\rho}\frac{\partial V}{\partial \phi}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi + \frac{\partial V}{\partial z}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$

Spherical Coordinates

$$\nabla V = \frac{\partial V}{\partial r}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_r + \frac{1}{r}\frac{\partial V}{\partial \theta}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\theta + \frac{1}{r\sin\theta}\frac{\partial V}{\partial \phi}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi$$

6. Divergence of a Vector Field ($\nabla \cdot \mathbf{A}$) & Divergence Theorem

Divergence represents the net outward flux of vector field $\mathbf{A}$ per unit volume as the volume shrinks to zero:

$$\text{div }\mathbf{A} = \nabla \cdot \mathbf{A} = \lim_{\Delta v\to 0}\frac{\oint_S \mathbf{A}\cdot d\mathbf{S}}{\Delta v}$$

Cartesian Coordinates

$$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{A} = \frac{\partial A_x}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial A_y}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial A_z}{\partial z}$$

Cylindrical Coordinates

$$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{A} = \frac{1}{\rho}\frac{\partial}{\partial \rho}(\rho A_\rho) + \frac{1}{\rho}\frac{\partial A_\phi}{\partial \phi} + \frac{\partial A_z}{\partial z}$$

Spherical Coordinates

$$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{A} = \frac{1}{r^2}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}(r^2 A_r) + \frac{1}{r\sin\theta}\frac{\partial}{\partial \theta}(A_\theta \sin\theta) + \frac{1}{r\sin\theta}\frac{\partial A_\phi}{\partial \phi}$$

Divergence Theorem (Gauss-Ostrogradsky Theorem)

The total outward flux of a vector field $\mathbf{A}$ through a closed surface $S$ equals the volume integral of the divergence of $\mathbf{A}$ inside volume $v$:
$$\oint_S \mathbf{A}\cdot d\mathbf{S} = \int_v (\nabla \cdot \mathbf{A})\,dv$$

7. Curl of a Vector Field ($\nabla \times \mathbf{A}$) & Stokes’ Theorem

The curl of $\mathbf{A}$ is an axial (rotational) vector representing the circulation of $\mathbf{A}$ per unit area:

$$\text{curl }\mathbf{A} = \nabla \times \mathbf{A} = \left(\lim_{\Delta S\to 0}\frac{\oint_L \mathbf{A}\cdot d\mathbf{l}}{\Delta S}\right)_{\max}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_n$$

Cartesian Coordinates

$$\nabla \times \mathbf{A} = \begin{vmatrix} \hat{\mathbf{a}}_x & \hat{\mathbf{a}}_y & \hat{\mathbf{a}}_z \\ \frac{\partial}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \\ A_x & A_y & A_z \end{vmatrix}$$ $$= \left(\frac{\partial A_z}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial A_y}{\partial z}\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_x + \left(\frac{\partial A_x}{\partial z} - \frac{\partial A_z}{\partial x}\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_y + \left(\frac{\partial A_y}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial A_x}{\partial y}\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$

Cylindrical Coordinates

$$\nabla \times \mathbf{A} = \frac{1}{\rho}\begin{vmatrix} \hat{\mathbf{a}}_\rho & \rho\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi & \hat{\mathbf{a}}_z \\ \frac{\partial}{\partial \rho} & \frac{\partial}{\partial \phi} & \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \\ A_\rho & \rho A_\phi & A_z \end{vmatrix}$$ $$= \left(\frac{1}{\rho}\frac{\partial A_z}{\partial \phi} - \frac{\partial A_\phi}{\partial z}\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\rho + \left(\frac{\partial A_\rho}{\partial z} - \frac{\partial A_z}{\partial \rho}\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi + \frac{1}{\rho}\left(\frac{\partial(\rho A_\phi)}{\partial \rho} - \frac{\partial A_\rho}{\partial \phi}\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$

Spherical Coordinates

$$\nabla \times \mathbf{A} = \frac{1}{r^2\sin\theta}\begin{vmatrix} \hat{\mathbf{a}}_r & r\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\theta & r\sin\theta\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi \\ \frac{\partial}{\partial r} & \frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} & \frac{\partial}{\partial \phi} \\ A_r & r A_\theta & r\sin\theta A_\phi \end{vmatrix}$$

Stokes’ Theorem

The circulation of a vector field $\mathbf{A}$ around a closed boundary contour $L$ equals the surface integral of the curl of $\mathbf{A}$ over open surface $S$ bounded by $L$:
$$\oint_L \mathbf{A}\cdot d\mathbf{l} = \int_S (\nabla \times \mathbf{A})\cdot d\mathbf{S}$$

8. Laplacian ($\nabla^2$) & Essential Vector Identities

Cartesian Laplacian of Scalar $V$

$$\nabla^2 V = \frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial x^2} + \frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial y^2} + \frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial z^2}$$

Cylindrical Laplacian of Scalar $V$

$$\nabla^2 V = \frac{1}{\rho}\frac{\partial}{\partial \rho}\left(\rho\frac{\partial V}{\partial \rho}\right) + \frac{1}{\rho^2}\frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial \phi^2} + \frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial z^2}$$

Spherical Laplacian of Scalar $V$

$$\nabla^2 V = \frac{1}{r^2}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}\left(r^2\frac{\partial V}{\partial r}\right) + \frac{1}{r^2\sin\theta}\frac{\partial}{\partial \theta}\left(\sin\theta\frac{\partial V}{\partial \theta}\right) + \frac{1}{r^2\sin^2\theta}\frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial \phi^2}$$

The 4 Null Identities (Exam Must-Knows)

  • Curl of Gradient is always Zero: $\nabla \times (\nabla V) \equiv 0$ (Every conservative / irrotational field is the gradient of a scalar).
  • Divergence of Curl is always Zero: $\nabla \cdot (\nabla \times \mathbf{A}) \equiv 0$ (Every solenoidal field is the curl of a vector potential).
  • Vector Laplacian: $\nabla^2 \mathbf{A} = \nabla(\nabla \cdot \mathbf{A}) - \nabla \times (\nabla \times \mathbf{A})$.
  • Helmholtz's Theorem: Any vector field is uniquely specified by both its divergence and its curl everywhere plus boundary conditions.

9. Worked Exam Problems

Example 1: Gradient of Scalar Fields in All 3 Systems

Find $\nabla V$ for:

  1. Cartesian: $V = e^{-z} \sin 2x \cosh y$ $$\nabla V = \left(2e^{-z}\cos 2x\cosh y\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_x + \left(e^{-z}\sin 2x\sinh y\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_y - \left(e^{-z}\sin 2x\cosh y\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$
  2. Cylindrical: $U = \rho^2 z \cos 2\phi$ $$\nabla U = \left(2\rho z\cos 2\phi\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\rho - \left(2\rho z\sin 2\phi\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi + \left(\rho^2\cos 2\phi\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$
  3. Spherical: $W = 10r\sin^2\theta\cos\phi$ $$\nabla W = \left(10\sin^2\theta\cos\phi\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_r + \left(10\sin 2\theta\cos\phi\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\theta - \left(10\sin\theta\sin\phi\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi$$
Example 2: Verification of Divergence Theorem on a Cylinder

Verify $\oint_S \mathbf{G}\cdot d\mathbf{S} = \int_v (\nabla\cdot\mathbf{G})\,dv$ for vector $\mathbf{G} = 10e^{-2z}(\rho\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\rho + \hat{\mathbf{a}}_z)$ over the cylinder $\rho = 1, \; 0 \le z \le 1$:

Surface Integral Approach:

  • Top face ($z = 1$): $\Psi_{\text{top}} = \int_{\phi=0}^{2\pi}\int_{\rho=0}^1 (10e^{-2})\rho\,d\rho\,d\phi = 10\pi e^{-2}$
  • Bottom face ($z = 0, d\mathbf{S} = -\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$): $\Psi_{\text{bottom}} = \int_{\phi=0}^{2\pi}\int_{\rho=0}^1 (-10)\rho\,d\rho\,d\phi = -10\pi$
  • Curved side ($\rho = 1, d\mathbf{S} = \hat{\mathbf{a}}_\rho$): $\Psi_{\text{side}} = \int_{\phi=0}^{2\pi}\int_{z=0}^1 10e^{-2z}(1)(1)\,dz\,d\phi = 2\pi(10)\left[\frac{e^{-2z}}{-2}\right]_0^1 = 10\pi(1 - e^{-2})$
$$\Psi_{\text{total}} = 10\pi e^{-2} - 10\pi + 10\pi - 10\pi e^{-2} = 0$$

Volume Integral via Divergence:

$$\nabla\cdot\mathbf{G} = \frac{1}{\rho}\frac{\partial}{\partial\rho}(\rho G_\rho) + \frac{\partial G_z}{\partial z} = \frac{1}{\rho}\frac{\partial}{\partial\rho}(\rho^2 10e^{-2z}) + \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(10e^{-2z}) = 20e^{-2z} - 20e^{-2z} = 0$$ $$\int_v (\nabla\cdot\mathbf{G})\,dv = \int_v 0\,dv = 0 \quad \checkmark \text{ Q.E.D.}$$
