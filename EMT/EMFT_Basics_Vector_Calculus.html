1. The Three Orthogonal Coordinate Systems

Cartesian $(x, y, z)$

Ranges: $-\infty \lt x \lt \infty,\ -\infty \lt y \lt \infty,\ -\infty \lt z \lt \infty$

$$d\mathbf{l}=dx\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_x+dy\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_y+dz\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$
$$dv=dx\,dy\,dz$$
$$d\mathbf{S}_x=dy\,dz\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_x$$
$$d\mathbf{S}_y=dx\,dz\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_y$$
$$d\mathbf{S}_z=dx\,dy\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$

Cylindrical $(\rho, \phi, z)$

Ranges: $0 \le \rho \lt \infty,\ 0 \le \phi \lt 2\pi,\ -\infty \lt z \lt \infty$

$$d\mathbf{l}=d\rho\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\rho+\rho\,d\phi\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi+dz\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$
$$dv=\rho\,d\rho\,d\phi\,dz$$
$$d\mathbf{S}_\rho=\rho\,d\phi\,dz\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\rho$$
$$d\mathbf{S}_\phi=d\rho\,dz\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi$$
$$d\mathbf{S}_z=\rho\,d\rho\,d\phi\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$

Spherical $(r, \theta, \phi)$

Ranges: $0 \le r \lt \infty,\ 0 \le \theta \le \pi,\ 0 \le \phi \lt 2\pi$

$$d\mathbf{l}=dr\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_r+r\,d\theta\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\theta+r\sin\theta\,d\phi\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi$$
$$dv=r^2\sin\theta\,dr\,d\theta\,d\phi$$
$$d\mathbf{S}_r=r^2\sin\theta\,d\theta\,d\phi\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_r$$
$$d\mathbf{S}_\theta=r\sin\theta\,dr\,d\phi\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\theta$$
$$d\mathbf{S}_\phi=r\,dr\,d\theta\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi$$

2. Coordinate & Vector Transformations

Cartesian ↔ Cylindrical

$$x=\rho\cos\phi,\quad y=\rho\sin\phi,\quad z=z$$
$$\rho=\sqrt{x^2+y^2},\quad \phi=\tan^{-1}(y/x)$$

Matrix transformation for vector components:

$$\begin{bmatrix}A_\rho\\A_\phi\\A_z\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\phi&\sin\phi&0\\-\sin\phi&\cos\phi&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}A_x\\A_y\\A_z\end{bmatrix}$$

Cartesian ↔ Spherical

$$x=r\sin\theta\cos\phi,\quad y=r\sin\theta\sin\phi,\quad z=r\cos\theta$$
$$r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2},\quad \theta=\cos^{-1}(z/r),\quad \phi=\tan^{-1}(y/x)$$

Matrix transformation for vector components:

$$\begin{bmatrix}A_r\\A_\theta\\A_\phi\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\sin\theta\cos\phi&\sin\theta\sin\phi&\cos\theta\\\cos\theta\cos\phi&\cos\theta\sin\phi&-\sin\theta\\-\sin\phi&\cos\phi&0\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}A_x\\A_y\\A_z\end{bmatrix}$$

3. Vector Line, Surface and Volume Integrals

Line integral

Circulation or work done along path $L$:

$$\int_L\mathbf{A}\cdot d\mathbf{l}$$

Closed line circulation: $\oint_L\mathbf{A}\cdot d\mathbf{l}$

Surface integral (flux)

Total outward flux $\Psi$ through surface $S$:

$$\Psi=\int_S\mathbf{A}\cdot d\mathbf{S}$$

Net outward flux from a closed surface: $\oint_S\mathbf{A}\cdot d\mathbf{S}$

Volume integral

Total charge $Q$ enclosed within volume $v$:

$$Q=\int_v\rho_v\,dv$$

4. The Vector Differential Del Operator ($\nabla$)

Cartesian

$$\nabla=\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_x+\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_y+\frac{\partial}{\partial z}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$

Cylindrical

$$\nabla=\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\rho\frac{\partial}{\partial\rho}+\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi\frac{1}{\rho}\frac{\partial}{\partial\phi}+\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z\frac{\partial}{\partial z}$$

Spherical

$$\nabla=\hat{\mathbf{a}}_r\frac{\partial}{\partial r}+\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\theta\frac{1}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta}+\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi\frac{1}{r\sin\theta}\frac{\partial}{\partial\phi}$$

5. Gradient of a Scalar Field ($\nabla V$)

The gradient of a scalar field $V$ is a vector that points in the direction of the maximum rate of increase of $V$, with magnitude equal to that maximum rate.

Cartesian

$$\nabla V=\frac{\partial V}{\partial x}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_x+\frac{\partial V}{\partial y}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_y+\frac{\partial V}{\partial z}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$

Cylindrical

$$\nabla V=\frac{\partial V}{\partial\rho}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\rho+\frac{1}{\rho}\frac{\partial V}{\partial\phi}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi+\frac{\partial V}{\partial z}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$

Spherical

$$\nabla V=\frac{\partial V}{\partial r}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_r+\frac{1}{r}\frac{\partial V}{\partial\theta}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\theta+\frac{1}{r\sin\theta}\frac{\partial V}{\partial\phi}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi$$

6. Divergence ($\nabla\cdot\mathbf{A}$) & the Divergence Theorem

Divergence is the net outward flux of $\mathbf{A}$ per unit volume as the volume shrinks to zero:

$$\text{div }\mathbf{A}=\nabla\cdot\mathbf{A}=\lim_{\Delta v\to0}\frac{\oint_S\mathbf{A}\cdot d\mathbf{S}}{\Delta v}$$

Cartesian

$$\nabla\cdot\mathbf{A}=\frac{\partial A_x}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial A_y}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial A_z}{\partial z}$$

Cylindrical

$$\nabla\cdot\mathbf{A}=\frac{1}{\rho}\frac{\partial}{\partial\rho}(\rho A_\rho)+\frac{1}{\rho}\frac{\partial A_\phi}{\partial\phi}+\frac{\partial A_z}{\partial z}$$

Spherical

$$\nabla\cdot\mathbf{A}=\frac{1}{r^2}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}(r^2A_r)+\frac{1}{r\sin\theta}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta}(A_\theta\sin\theta)+\frac{1}{r\sin\theta}\frac{\partial A_\phi}{\partial\phi}$$

Divergence theorem (Gauss–Ostrogradsky)

The total outward flux of $\mathbf{A}$ through a closed surface $S$ equals the volume integral of the divergence of $\mathbf{A}$ inside volume $v$:

$$\oint_S\mathbf{A}\cdot d\mathbf{S}=\int_v(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{A})\,dv$$

7. Curl ($\nabla\times\mathbf{A}$) & Stokes' Theorem

The curl of $\mathbf{A}$ is an axial (rotational) vector representing the circulation of $\mathbf{A}$ per unit area:

$$\text{curl }\mathbf{A}=\nabla\times\mathbf{A}=\left(\lim_{\Delta S\to0}\frac{\oint_L\mathbf{A}\cdot d\mathbf{l}}{\Delta S}\right)_{\max}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_n$$

Cartesian

$$\nabla\times\mathbf{A}=\begin{vmatrix}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_x&\hat{\mathbf{a}}_y&\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z\\\frac{\partial}{\partial x}&\frac{\partial}{\partial y}&\frac{\partial}{\partial z}\\A_x&A_y&A_z\end{vmatrix}$$
$$=\left(\frac{\partial A_z}{\partial y}-\frac{\partial A_y}{\partial z}\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_x+\left(\frac{\partial A_x}{\partial z}-\frac{\partial A_z}{\partial x}\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_y+\left(\frac{\partial A_y}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial A_x}{\partial y}\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$

Cylindrical

$$\nabla\times\mathbf{A}=\frac{1}{\rho}\begin{vmatrix}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\rho&\rho\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi&\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z\\\frac{\partial}{\partial\rho}&\frac{\partial}{\partial\phi}&\frac{\partial}{\partial z}\\A_\rho&\rho A_\phi&A_z\end{vmatrix}$$
$$=\left(\frac{1}{\rho}\frac{\partial A_z}{\partial\phi}-\frac{\partial A_\phi}{\partial z}\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\rho+\left(\frac{\partial A_\rho}{\partial z}-\frac{\partial A_z}{\partial\rho}\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi+\frac{1}{\rho}\left(\frac{\partial(\rho A_\phi)}{\partial\rho}-\frac{\partial A_\rho}{\partial\phi}\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$

Spherical

$$\nabla\times\mathbf{A}=\frac{1}{r^2\sin\theta}\begin{vmatrix}\hat{\mathbf{a}}_r&r\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\theta&r\sin\theta\,\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi\\\frac{\partial}{\partial r}&\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta}&\frac{\partial}{\partial\phi}\\A_r&rA_\theta&r\sin\theta\,A_\phi\end{vmatrix}$$

Expanded:

$$=\frac{1}{r\sin\theta}\left(\frac{\partial(A_\phi\sin\theta)}{\partial\theta}-\frac{\partial A_\theta}{\partial\phi}\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_r+\frac{1}{r}\left(\frac{1}{\sin\theta}\frac{\partial A_r}{\partial\phi}-\frac{\partial(rA_\phi)}{\partial r}\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\theta+\frac{1}{r}\left(\frac{\partial(rA_\theta)}{\partial r}-\frac{\partial A_r}{\partial\theta}\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi$$

Stokes' theorem

The circulation of $\mathbf{A}$ around a closed contour $L$ equals the surface integral of the curl of $\mathbf{A}$ over any open surface $S$ bounded by $L$:

$$\oint_L\mathbf{A}\cdot d\mathbf{l}=\int_S(\nabla\times\mathbf{A})\cdot d\mathbf{S}$$

8. Laplacian ($\nabla^2$) & Essential Identities

Cartesian Laplacian of scalar $V$

$$\nabla^2V=\frac{\partial^2V}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2V}{\partial y^2}+\frac{\partial^2V}{\partial z^2}$$

Cylindrical Laplacian of scalar $V$

$$\nabla^2V=\frac{1}{\rho}\frac{\partial}{\partial\rho}\left(\rho\frac{\partial V}{\partial\rho}\right)+\frac{1}{\rho^2}\frac{\partial^2V}{\partial\phi^2}+\frac{\partial^2V}{\partial z^2}$$

Spherical Laplacian of scalar $V$

$$\nabla^2V=\frac{1}{r^2}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}\left(r^2\frac{\partial V}{\partial r}\right)+\frac{1}{r^2\sin\theta}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta}\left(\sin\theta\frac{\partial V}{\partial\theta}\right)+\frac{1}{r^2\sin^2\theta}\frac{\partial^2V}{\partial\phi^2}$$

Key identities (exam must-knows)

  • Curl of gradient is always zero: $\nabla\times(\nabla V)\equiv0$. Every conservative (irrotational) field is the gradient of a scalar.
  • Divergence of curl is always zero: $\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf{A})\equiv0$. Every solenoidal field is the curl of a vector potential.
  • Vector Laplacian: $\nabla^2\mathbf{A}=\nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{A})-\nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf{A})$.
  • Helmholtz's theorem: any vector field is uniquely specified by both its divergence and its curl everywhere, plus boundary conditions.

9. Worked Exam Problems

Example 1: Gradient of scalar fields in all 3 systems

Find $\nabla V$ for:

  1. Cartesian: $V=e^{-z}\sin2x\cosh y$
    $$\nabla V=\left(2e^{-z}\cos2x\cosh y\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_x+\left(e^{-z}\sin2x\sinh y\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_y-\left(e^{-z}\sin2x\cosh y\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$
  2. Cylindrical: $U=\rho^2z\cos2\phi$
    $$\nabla U=\left(2\rho z\cos2\phi\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\rho-\left(2\rho z\sin2\phi\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi+\left(\rho^2\cos2\phi\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$$
  3. Spherical: $W=10r\sin^2\theta\cos\phi$
    $$\nabla W=\left(10\sin^2\theta\cos\phi\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_r+\left(10\sin2\theta\cos\phi\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\theta-\left(10\sin\theta\sin\phi\right)\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\phi$$
Example 2: Verification of the divergence theorem on a cylinder

Verify $\oint_S\mathbf{G}\cdot d\mathbf{S}=\int_v(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{G})\,dv$ for $\mathbf{G}=10e^{-2z}(\rho\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\rho+\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z)$ over the cylinder $\rho=1,\ 0\le z\le1$.

Surface integral approach:

  • Top face ($z=1$): $\Psi_{\text{top}}=\int_{\phi=0}^{2\pi}\int_{\rho=0}^{1}(10e^{-2})\rho\,d\rho\,d\phi=10\pi e^{-2}$
  • Bottom face ($z=0$, $d\mathbf{S}=-\hat{\mathbf{a}}_z$): $\Psi_{\text{bottom}}=\int_{\phi=0}^{2\pi}\int_{\rho=0}^{1}(-10)\rho\,d\rho\,d\phi=-10\pi$
  • Curved side ($\rho=1$, $d\mathbf{S}=\hat{\mathbf{a}}_\rho$): $\Psi_{\text{side}}=\int_{\phi=0}^{2\pi}\int_{z=0}^{1}10e^{-2z}\,dz\,d\phi=2\pi(10)\left[\dfrac{e^{-2z}}{-2}\right]_0^1=10\pi(1-e^{-2})$
$$\Psi_{\text{total}}=10\pi e^{-2}-10\pi+10\pi-10\pi e^{-2}=0$$

Volume integral via divergence:

$$\nabla\cdot\mathbf{G}=\frac{1}{\rho}\frac{\partial}{\partial\rho}\left(\rho^2\,10e^{-2z}\right)+\frac{\partial}{\partial z}\left(10e^{-2z}\right)=20e^{-2z}-20e^{-2z}=0$$
$$\int_v(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{G})\,dv=\int_v0\,dv=0\quad\checkmark\ \text{Q.E.D.}$$
