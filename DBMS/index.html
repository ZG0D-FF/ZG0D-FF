🎓 7. Course Curriculum & Outcomes (PCCEC504)

Subject: Database Management Systems
Code: PCCEC504
Credits: 3 (36 Lecture Hours)
Evaluation: 100 Total Marks (Internal: 30 · Semester Exam: 70)

Course Outcomes (COs)

  • CO1: Database Architecture & Modeling: Enterprise database system design, 3-schema architecture, data independence, and conceptual ER modeling.
  • CO2: Relational Theory & SQL Mastery: Logical schema design utilizing formal relational algebra and calculus, extending into declarative SQL queries.
  • CO3: Relational Schema Refinement: Functional dependencies, Armstrong axioms, canonical covers, and normalization algorithms (1NF through BCNF).
  • CO4: Systems Internals & Storage: Transaction schedules, ACID properties, concurrency control (2PL), recovery, and physical B+ Tree indexing.
