01 Rainforest

The Rainforest
Realm

Lungs of the Earth

Dense, vibrant ecosystems filled with towering trees, rich biodiversity, and constant life cycles.

🐅 Tiger 🐒 Monkey 🦜 Parrot 🐆 Jaguar
  • Rainforests cover only 6% of Earth but hold over 50% of species
  • They produce 20% of the world's oxygen
  • Home to millions of undiscovered species
02 Ocean

The Ocean
Depths

A Hidden Universe

Vast, mysterious waters covering most of our planet, filled with life from coral reefs to deep-sea creatures.

🐋 Whale 🦈 Shark 🐙 Octopus 🪼 Jellyfish
  • Oceans cover over 70% of Earth's surface
  • More than 80% of the ocean remains unexplored
  • The deepest point plunges over 11,000 meters
03 Desert

The Desert
Expanse

Survival Against Odds

Harsh, dry landscapes where only the toughest life forms endure extreme heat and scarcity.

🐪 Camel 🐍 Snake 🦂 Scorpion 🦊 Fox
  • Deserts receive less than 250mm of rain annually
  • Some deserts are cold, not hot
  • Desert plants store water for survival
04 Arctic

The Arctic
Frontier

Frozen but Alive

Icy landscapes with extreme cold, yet rich ecosystems adapted to survive the harshest climates on Earth.

🐻‍❄️ Polar Bear 🦭 Seal 🐧 Penguin
  • Temperatures can drop below −50°C
  • Ice reflects sunlight, regulating Earth's temperature
  • Wildlife depends on seasonal ice cycles
05 Savanna

The Savanna
Plains

The Circle of Life

Open grasslands where predators and prey coexist in a delicate, ancient balance.

🦁 Lion 🐘 Elephant 🦓 Zebra 🦒 Giraffe
  • Savannas have distinct wet and dry seasons
  • Fires help maintain ecosystem balance
  • Home to Earth's largest land animals
