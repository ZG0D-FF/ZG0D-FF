Long Read

How atmospheric water harvesting became the technology that could save the next century.

In the Atacama — the driest non-polar desert on Earth — engineers have built fog nets that wring drinking water from air. The same principle now scales to fog-free environments using cold surfaces and hygroscopic membranes. Chile, Morocco, and Oman have operational municipal systems. The question is no longer whether it works. It is whether it will scale fast enough.
